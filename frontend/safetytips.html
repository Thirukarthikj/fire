Pyrotechnic Safety Code

The Golden Rules: What To Do & What To Avoid

Follow these time-tested protocols to safeguard your family, neighbors, pets, and property during your Diwali celebrations.

Essential Safety Practices (DOs)

Always follow these rules before lighting fireworks

  • Ignite in Clear, Open Outdoor Grounds

    Always burst crackers on hard, flat open ground away from parked cars, dry grass, sheds, and trees.

  • Use Long Agarbatti or Sparkler from an Arm's Length

    Never lean over the firework. Light the tip of the fuse from an arm's distance and immediately walk backwards 5 meters.

  • Keep Two Buckets of Water & Sand on Standby

    Ensure two buckets filled with fresh tap water and dry sand are physically placed next to the firing perimeter.

  • Wear Snug-Fitting, Pure Cotton Clothing

    Dress in cotton garments with closed-toe footwear. Avoid synthetic fibers, loose dupattas, and flowing sarees near sparks.

  • Maintain 100% Constant Adult Supervision

    Children must never handle fireworks unsupervised. Equip young children with protective eye goggles.

  • Submerge Burnt Sparkler Wires in Water Immediately

    Hot sparkler wires retain 500°C+ residual heat. Drop spent wires straight into a water bucket to avoid foot burns.

Critical Hazards to Strictly Avoid (DON'Ts)

Zero-tolerance dangerous practices

  • Never Relight or Inspect "Dud" Crackers

    If a firework fails to go off, wait at least 15 minutes, keep your distance, and pour water over it from a safe range.

  • Never Hold Crackers in Hand While Lighting

    Always place fireworks flat on the ground. Never throw ignited crackers towards other people, roads, or balconies.

  • Never Put Fireworks Inside Glass Bottles or Metal Cans

    Confining crackers inside containers causes violent fragmentation, creating lethal high-velocity shrapnel.

  • Never Carry Fireworks Inside Pants or Shirt Pockets

    Friction, body heat, and accidental static charges can ignite sensitive black powder fuses instantly.

  • Never Burst Indoors, on Balconies, or Under Awnings

    Enclosed spaces trap high-decibel shockwaves, toxic fumes, and increase indoor residential fire hazards.

  • Never Use Matchsticks or Short Cigarette Lighters

    Short open flames position your hands and face dangerously close to the rapid explosive flash zone.

Category Guidelines

Safety Tips by Cracker Category

Different pyrotechnic compositions require specific launching instructions.

Flower Pots & Ground Chakkars

High-temperature colorful sparks with rapid rotational force. Requires flat, firm masonry or tarmac ground.

  • Light only the uppermost fuse tip obliquely
  • Maintain a 5-meter safety perimeter
  • Never place on loose sand or sloped grass

Aerial Sky Shots & Multi-Shot Cakes

Vertical projectile fireworks with exploding starbursts. Stability at the launching base is critical.

  • Secure the base with heavy bricks or sandbags
  • Verify zero overhead wires, trees, or balconies
  • Keep spectators at least 15 meters away

Sparklers & Kids Novelties

Gentle festive sparkles that reach very high wire temperatures. Ideal for family celebrations when guided.

  • Hold sparklers horizontally away from body
  • Only one sparkler per child at a time
  • Drop hot spent wires directly into water
Official Emergency Protocol

In Case of Accidental Thermal Burns

Act swiftly and calmly. Follow these medical guidelines approved by trauma physicians to minimize tissue damage.

01

Cool with Running Water

Immediately hold the burned area under clean, cool running tap water for 10–15 continuous minutes. Never use ice or ice-cold water.

02

Strictly Avoid Home Remedies

Never apply ink, toothpaste, turmeric, butter, oil, or ghee. These trap heat inside the skin and cause dangerous deep infections.

03

Cover Loosely with Clean Cloth

Gently drape a sterile non-stick gauze pad or clean, dry cotton cloth over the burn. Never pop blisters or apply adhesive tape.

04

Seek Medical Assistance

For burns to the face, eyes, hands, or if severe blistering occurs, proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Compassionate Celebration

Caring for Pets, Infants & Senior Citizens

Animals and infants possess hearing sensitivity up to 4 times sharper than adults. Keep Diwali joyous for everyone in your household with these thoughtful measures:

  • Keep domestic pets indoors inside a sound-dampened room during evening peak hours.
  • Play soft background music or keep television on to mask external sharp acoustic peaks.
  • Close windows and draw curtains to reduce explosive flashes and acoustic decibels.
  • Ensure pets wear collar tags with current contact numbers in case they startle and run outside.
