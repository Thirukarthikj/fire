OUR ROOTS & CRAFTSMANSHIP

From the Heart of Sivakasi to Every Indian Living Room

Established in 1998 in Sivakasi — India's world-renowned pyrotechnic capital — Siva's Pyro Paradise was founded on a simple, enduring conviction: that every family festival deserves to be illuminated with breathtaking brilliance, uncompromising safety, and genuine wholesale value.

Over two and a half decades, our master pyrotechnicians have preserved Sivakasi's artisanal crafting traditions while pioneering the nation's transition into modern eco-friendly green crackers. Every product — from vibrant ground chakkars and dazzling flower pots to sky-commanding multi-shot aerials — is formulated without harmful heavy metals like Barium nitrate.

By eliminating multiple layers of intermediaries, distributors, and seasonal markups, we deliver authentic Sivakasi factory-direct prices straight to your doorstep across India.

  • Direct Factory Wholesale Rates: Transparent savings up to 70% off standard retail MRP.
  • 100% CSIR-NEERI Green Chemistry: 30-35% reduced emissions with official QR code tracking.
  • Multi-Stage Safety Testing: Precise ignition fuse timings and decibel compliance under 125 dB.
  • Pan-India Express Surface Transport: Secure packaging in moisture-proof fire-retardant cartons.
